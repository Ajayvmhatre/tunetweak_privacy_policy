Privacy Policy

Tune Tweak  ·  Package: com.tunetweak.player  ·  Last updated: 23 September 2025

Short version: Tune Tweak is an offline practice player. Your songs, recordings and settings stay on your device. There is no account, no login, and we do not collect or sell any personal data.

1. Data stored on your device (never uploaded)

  • Audio files you open or import, and the playlists / folders you create.
  • Recordings you make inside the app.
  • App settings (volume, speed, pitch, tuner preference, ad-sound preference).

This data is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Microphone — for the Tuner, the Pitch Meter and Recording. Audio is analysed in real time on the device and is never uploaded.
  • Music & audio / storage — to find and play songs already on your phone, and to save exported files.

Both permissions are optional: the app still opens and plays without them (you can pick files with the system file picker).

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Services / Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — when advertisements are shown, Google may collect device or other IDs, approximate location and app interactions for advertising and measurement, according to Google’s own privacy policy. You can remove ads entirely with the Premium subscription.
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscriptions (₹29 / month, ₹199 / year). Payment details are handled by Google, not by us.
  • RevenueCat — stores only the purchase status needed to unlock Premium on this device.

No song audio, recording or file name is ever sent to these services.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone. Ads are served by Google, which applies its own family policies.

5. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all on-device data. Because we never collect personal data on servers, there is nothing else to delete. To reset the ad identifier, use Android Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
